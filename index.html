A quick card game

Slap Jack

Win all 52 cards by being the first to slap each Jack.

How to play

  1. You and the computer take turns playing a card.
  2. When a Jack appears, hit SLAP! before the computer.
  3. A correct slap wins the center pile. A wrong slap costs you a card.

First to collect the deck wins
