Slap Jack

How to Play

Play cards, watch for Jacks, and be faster than the computer.

  1. Click Play Card to play your top card.
  2. When a Jack appears, click SLAP! before the computer.
  3. A correct slap adds the center pile to your Cards Won.
  4. An incorrect slap costs one of your remaining cards.
  5. After all 52 cards are played, whoever has won the most cards wins.
